About KomaKode

Practical software built independently

KomaKode is an independent software-development company focused on practical software for Apple platforms.

What KomaKode builds

KomaKode products are focused tools for specific workflows across development, personal finance, baseball scoring, and meal planning.

DevDoctor

macOS development-environment diagnostics, currently in development.

DebtScope

Debt payoff and financial planning for iPhone and iPad.

ScoreKeep

Baseball scoring, documentation, and MLB roster resources.

PlateWise

AI-assisted recipes and meal planning.

Development approach

KomaKode emphasizes practical solutions to specific problems, native Apple-platform experiences, clear and maintainable design, and privacy-conscious handling of user information.

The website follows a static-first, low-cost approach with no unnecessary infrastructure.

Independent development

Independent software development means focused products and direct responsibility for design, implementation, maintenance, and support decisions.

That approach keeps product scope tied to real user workflows while avoiding claims or systems that are not needed for the current products.
