macOS Developer Utility

DevDoctor

A native macOS diagnostic utility designed to help developers understand and troubleshoot their development environment.

Status In development

Overview

DevDoctor is being developed for Mac software developers who need clearer information when diagnosing development environment problems.

The product is focused on developer context rather than general-purpose consumer Mac repair.

Key Benefits

  • Native macOS experience
  • Focus on the software-development environment
  • Clearer diagnostic and troubleshooting context
  • Direct distribution when released

Availability

DevDoctor is in development. Direct distribution is planned, but it is not currently available for purchase or download.

System Requirements

Final system requirements will be published before release.

Resources

Screenshots and demonstrations will be added as development progresses.

Release notes and product support information will be published before release.
