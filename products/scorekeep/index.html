iPhone and iPad Baseball Scoring

ScoreKeep

ScoreKeep helps baseball fans and scorekeepers record games, manage lineups, and track game statistics.

View ScoreKeep on the App Store Open the ScoreKeep manual

Overview

ScoreKeep is a practical baseball scoring application for recording games and keeping roster, lineup, player, and game information organized.

Key Capabilities

Baseball game scoring

Record baseball games from an iPhone or iPad.

Rosters and lineups

Manage team rosters and lineups for scoring workflows.

Statistics

Track player and game statistics as part of the scoring process.

Importable teams

Use importable MLB team roster files provided through the existing home-page roster-download experience.

Availability

ScoreKeep is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.

Resources

The ScoreKeep manual is available as a PDF. MLB team roster downloads remain available through the existing home-page interface.
