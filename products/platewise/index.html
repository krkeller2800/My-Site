AI-Assisted Meal Planning

PlateWise

PlateWise is an AI-assisted recipe and meal-planning application for organizing recipes, choosing meals, and preparing shopping lists.

View PlateWise on the App Store PlateWise support information

Overview

PlateWise helps keep meal planning practical by combining personal recipes, pantry context, meal plans, and shopping lists.

Key Capabilities

  • Store and organize personal recipes
  • Find or generate recipe ideas with AI assistance
  • Use pantry information to help choose meals
  • Plan meals
  • Build shopping lists

Availability

PlateWise meal planning is available on the App Store.
